Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE refined geometric SPHERICAL identity symbol for a product designer working with computational form, material systems and 3D configurators. The previous proposal was simply a black circle cut in two; the owner wants the spherical idea to become MORE INTRICATE and architecturally constructed. Create a compact spherical silhouette assembled from FOUR interlocking curved shell panels. Use two deliberately different oblique curved seams and one short transverse seam, generous white negative-space channels, precise circular outer contour and optically balanced panel proportions. The panels should suggest a solid three-dimensional object that can be disassembled and reconfigured, while the actual artwork is strictly flat and monochrome. Sophisticated spatial geometry; a memorable cut-and-assembled sphere, not a striped globe. Show exactly one large centered final symbol on a pure white background. Solid BLACK shapes only, crisp vector-quality edges. The mark should still hold up as a 24px icon. No name, initials, text, typography, labels, gradients, shading, shadows, texture, presentation mockups, extra shapes, star, spark, torus, atom orbits, Saturn ring, yin-yang, flower, tree, conventional globe grid or parallel latitude stripes. The outer silhouette must remain a recognisable sphere. Restrained but inventive, timeless industrial-design identity.
